How Earn works — where are my funds? · Risks · APY math
Your funds are deposited directly into the protocol's smart contracts. Noise Hub does not custody them. APY is variable and may change. Risks: smart-contract, market, liquidity, protocol, impermanent loss where applicable. Estimate: annual = principal × APY; period = principal × APY × days/365.
