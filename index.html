HOW IT WORKS

From intent
to confirmation.

Noise is designed around a simple boundary: the hub can prepare and simulate, but the user's wallet authorizes the transaction.

01

Connect

Connect a Sui wallet. Noise works with the address context without taking custody.

02

Discover

Inspect live markets, assets, opportunities and supported protocol data.

03

Compare

Review market data, quotes, fees and available execution paths.

04

Simulate

Where supported, simulation gates the transaction before the wallet is prompted.

05

Sign

Your wallet remains the authorization boundary. Noise never receives your private key.

06

Track

Follow the transaction through submission, confirmation or failure in Activity.

CONTROL & TRANSPARENCY

Built to show the boundary.

Wallet controlled

Signing stays with the connected wallet. Noise does not hold private keys or seed phrases.

Simulation first

Supported transaction paths use simulation gates before the wallet authorization step.

Fees are explicit

Protocol fees and any configured Noise service fee are separated and shown with their applicable units.

Live sources

Market and ecosystem information is sourced from integrated providers and marked when unavailable.

No fake activity

Unavailable feeds remain unavailable instead of being replaced with fabricated market statistics.

Transaction state

Activity follows the transaction lifecycle so users can see what happened after signing.
