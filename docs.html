Connected, non-custodial Sui operations. Updated 2026-10-09. Unconnected projects and trading modes are not offered in the public app.

Unsigned builders and simulations are tested. This is not an independent security audit; no funded wallet signatures or live cross-chain transfers were performed during the audit.

How it works

Choose → Review → Build → Simulate → Wallet signs → Track

Your wallet is the signing authority. Ordinary hub Sui transactions require a fresh successful simulation and matching account/network before the wallet prompt. The official Wormhole widget handles its own cross-chain routing, signing and recovery.

Portfolio

Live coin balances, protocol positions and native staking. Missing price or position valuations show unavailable; they are not invented or inferred from a coin count. Deposited tokens must not also be counted as spendable wallet coins.

Discover

Public catalogue contains only connected operations, not the entire ecosystem. Market data includes source and update time. A failed API shows unavailable, not a fabricated APR/APY.

Swap

Cetus Aggregator, Aftermath, Turbos, FlowX, Momentum and Bluefin Spot. Selected DEX routes are restricted and checked against the requested venue. The hub fee is carved from the input amount, not charged on top. Both the quote and build use the net route input. Builds refresh quotes.

Earn and Kai

Kai SUI/USDC single-asset vault deposits and y-token redemptions use the official SDK and current vault state. No leveraged Kai position UI is offered. SDK APR/APY ratios are multiplied by 100 for percentage display. Yield is variable; performance fees and available withdrawal liquidity apply.

Staking

Native SUI stake/unstake, Aftermath afSUI, Haedal, Volo, SpringSui and Metastable mSUI supported operations. Minimum deposits, redemption delays and provider fees depend on the live protocol. No fixed returns are promised.

Lending

NAVI, Suilend and Scallop supply/withdraw/borrow/repay; NAVI/Suilend reward claims. Health and liquidity constraints are checked when relevant; on-chain simulation is mandatory. Bucket PSM stable swaps are separate from a savings-vault deposit.

Liquidity

Aftermath and STEAMM pool deposit/withdraw. Live pool IDs and coin metadata, exact base units and quoted slippage minima are used. Cetus concentrated-liquidity deposits are not offered. Impermanent loss, protocol fees and withdrawal liquidity risk apply.

Trading

DeepBook spot market/limit orders, order cancellation and BalanceManager setup. DeepBook Predict is available only for active protocol markets. Leveraged margin and other perps/options platforms are removed from the public trading interface.

Bridge

Official self-hosted Wormhole Connect 6.0.0 inside Noise. Available networks are Sui, Ethereum, Arbitrum, Base and Solana; routes and assets are determined by the official widget, not assumed to exist between every pair. Read route fees, estimated destination output and token identity before signing. Use widget history/recovery to finish an incomplete transfer. Source/destination wallets can differ. No Noise bridge commission. Native Sui Bridge is not presented as connected.

Security

  • Never paste a seed phrase or private key into Noise.
  • Wallet account and network checks; failed/unavailable Sui simulation blocks signing.
  • No automatic spending or claimed $100/$500 security limits.
  • Simulation cannot guarantee safety against protocol exploits or later market changes.
  • Bridge security and recovery follow Wormhole's official implementation.

Fees

Default Noise router fee: 2 bps (0.02%) in the input asset, deducted from the amount routed. Pool fees are already reflected in output; network gas is separately denominated in SUI. Do not add unlike assets into a misleading total. Lending, staking, LP, Kai, Bucket PSM and bridge add no Noise fee by default.

Default recipient: 0xa29a8f72981c5644c348a51cd4aded6dbb47ad4361f7a825e19d377e9c4373a1. Server environment or validated database configuration may override it. No client-provided recipient is accepted for a hub fee. The built transaction and Review expose the effective recipient.

A zero address balance is not proof of lifetime revenue; arbitrary deposits are not proof of Noise commission. Historical commission receipts require confirmed transaction evidence. Referral tracks signed invitations and confirmed Workflows only; payouts and automated spending are not implemented.

FAQ

Can I earn a guaranteed APY? No. Rates and losses depend on third-party protocols and market conditions.

Workflows

Atomic swap + deposit for Suilend, NAVI, Kai, Scallop, Haedal, SpringSui, Metastable mSUI and Volo. Receipt verification and position refresh follow signing. Withdrawal is separately reviewed and signed. No universal atomic exit or automatic spending is promised.

Invitations and AI

Referral requires personal-message ownership proof and counts confirmed workflows after attribution, not earnings. Leaderboard counts receipts for Noise-prepared workflow digests, not all Sui activity. No payout is implied.

AI live tools are read-only. Language-model answers require a server-side key and model; otherwise tools-only responses are clearly labelled. SuiPump is indexer discovery and optional DEX-route checking, not native bonding-curve trading.
